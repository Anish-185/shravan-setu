# Volunteer consent form: team test of a school hearing screening app

**What this is.** We are a student team building a free phone app that helps schools find children who may need an
ear check, and makes sure they reach a health centre. Before any school uses it, we test the app on adult volunteers.
**This is not a hearing test of you, and it gives no diagnosis.**

**What you will do (about 15 minutes, in two short visits).**
- Wear wired earphones and tap a button whenever you hear a short beep. Some beeps are very faint.
- Visit 1: about 8 minutes (this form + one listening run). Visit 2: about 6 to 9 minutes (two or three short app tests).
- We may ask you to tap in a set way on purpose (for example, tapping at random) to test the app.

**Who can take part.** Adults aged 18 or over, with no known hearing problem, and no cold, blocked nose, ear pain or
ear discharge today.

**Risks.** Very low. The beeps are short and mostly quiet. If any sound feels uncomfortable, take the earphones off;
we will stop.

**What we record.** A code (like V3), not your name. Your taps, the beep levels you heard, how long each test took,
room noise numbers (never a recording of sound), and the phone and earphone model. Your name appears only on this paper
form, which the team lead keeps and does not scan or upload.

**How we use it.** To set the app's sound levels and to check that it gives the same result twice. Summary numbers
(never names) may appear in our challenge submission, labelled as a small team test with adults.

**Your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. If you stop, you may ask
us to delete your results by quoting your code.
